The best colours for fair and pale skin

Short answer: fair skin looks best in soft to medium colours that don't overpower it. Think navy, soft blue, sage, dusty rose, lavender and berry for cool skin, and coral, peach, camel and warm green for warm skin. Be careful with stark black, neon and beige or nude shades that match your skin.

Why softer shades win

Very intense colours can dominate fair skin, so people notice the clothes before your face. Medium-strength colours and gentle contrast balance it. You can still wear dark colours: navy and charcoal are usually kinder than pure black.

Colours to be careful with

  • Pure black right at your face. It can make fair skin look washed out or tired. Try navy, charcoal or chocolate instead.
  • Neon and very bright yellow or orange, which overpower pale skin.
  • Nude, beige and pale pink close to your skin colour, unless you add contrast with a darker piece.

Love a colour that isn't on your list? Wear it away from your face, as trousers, shoes or a bag.

Easy outfit ideas

  • Navy knit + light jeans + white trainers
  • Soft blue shirt + stone chinos
  • Sage top + cream trousers
  • Berry or burgundy dress + silver jewellery (cool skin)
  • Camel coat + cream roll-neck (warm skin)

Quick answers

What colours look good on pale skin?

Soft and medium colours: navy, soft blue, sage, dusty rose, lavender, berry and burgundy suit cool pale skin, while coral, peach, camel and warm greens suit warm pale skin.

Should pale people wear black?

You can, but pure black right next to your face can look harsh. Navy, charcoal or chocolate brown give a similar look and are usually more flattering.

What colours make pale skin look tanned?

Mid-tone warm colours like coral, terracotta and teal make fair skin look warmer and healthier. White and pastels can make it look paler.
