Colour guide

The best colours for medium and olive skin

Short answer: medium and olive skin suits rich, mid-depth colours such as teal, terracotta, olive, burgundy, mustard and sapphire. Earth tones flatter warm and olive skin; jewel tones flatter cooler medium skin. Avoid pale yellows and beiges that sit close to your skin colour, which can make you look washed out.

Olive skin is often neutral-to-warm

Olive skin has a green or grey cast that confuses most undertone tests: veins can look both blue and green. That's normal. Earth tones and deep jewel tones usually win, and very bright neon or pale pastels usually lose.

Colours to be careful with

  • Beige, nude and pale yellow close to your skin colour. They blend in and drain your face.
  • Neon brights. They tend to overpower medium skin and make it look sallow.
  • Grey right next to your face, especially for olive skin. Choose navy or charcoal instead.

Love a colour that isn't on your list? Wear it away from your face, as trousers, shoes or a bag.

Easy outfit ideas

  • Olive overshirt + white tee + dark jeans

Quick answers

What colours look best on olive skin?

Earth tones and jewel tones: olive, khaki, terracotta, burgundy, teal, emerald and mustard. Navy and cream are the best neutrals.

What colours should olive skin avoid?

Pale yellows, beiges close to your skin colour, and very bright neons, which can make olive skin look sallow or washed out.

Is olive skin warm or cool?

Usually neutral to warm, but the green tint can make it read as cool. If both gold and silver suit you, treat yourself as neutral.
