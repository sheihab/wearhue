Popular questions

What colours look good on brown skin?

Bright, rich colours: cobalt blue, emerald, red, orange, mustard and fuchsia, plus crisp white and cream. Avoid browns that match your own skin. You'll blend in. Full guide for dark & brown skin →

What colours suit pale skin?

Soft to medium shades: navy, soft blue, sage, dusty rose and berry. Coral and camel suit warm pale skin. Pure black at your face can look harsh; navy or charcoal is kinder. Full guide for fair skin →

What colours suit olive skin?

Earth and jewel tones: olive, terracotta, teal, burgundy, emerald and mustard. Pale yellow and beige close to your skin colour can wash you out. Full guide for medium & olive skin →

Am I warm or cool toned?

Green wrist veins, gold jewellery suiting you and tanning easily point to warm. Blue or purple veins, silver and burning easily point to cool. A mix means neutral. 4 quick undertone tests →

What colour goes with everything?

Neutrals: white, black, grey, navy, beige and denim. Pair any one colour with them and it works. Keep outfits to 2–3 main colours. How to match clothes colours →
