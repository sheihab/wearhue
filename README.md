# Wearhue: colours that love your skin

**👉 Try it free: [sheihab.github.io/wearhue](https://sheihab.github.io/wearhue/)**

Wearhue is a free colour analysis web app. Pick your skin tone (or take a selfie; it's analysed on your device and never uploaded), answer three quick undertone questions, and get:

- **Your best clothing colours**, neutrals, colours to go easy on, and jewellery metals
- **Ready-made outfit combinations** built from your palette
- **"Does it match?"**: pick any two colours and see if they work together, and on you

No sign-up. Works on phones and laptops, and installs like an app on Android, Windows and Mac.
