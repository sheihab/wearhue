Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

Wearhue doesn't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email to use it. If you choose a selfie, it's processed only in your browser. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What's stored

Your chosen skin shade and undertone result are saved in your own browser (localStorage) so you can see your palette next time. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep Wearhue free. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Learn more at how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Shopping links

Some "Shop this colour" links may be affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Email list (optional)

If you join our email list, your email is handled by the form or email provider linked on the site. It's used only to send Wearhue updates, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Contact

Questions? Email hello.wearhue@gmail.com.
