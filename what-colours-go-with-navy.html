Colour matching

What colours go with navy?

Short answer: Navy goes with white, light blue, grey, camel, burgundy, pink and mustard. White and camel are the safest; burgundy and mustard add colour without clashing.

7 colours that go with navy

Navy + WhiteThe cleanest, sharpest combination: navy chinos with a white shirt or tee, or a navy blazer over white.
Navy + Light blueTonal and smart. The classic navy suit with a light blue shirt.
Navy + Camel / tanWarm and rich: camel coat over navy, or tan shoes and a belt with navy trousers.
Navy + GreyCalm and professional. A grey knit with navy trousers works for work or uni.
Navy + BurgundyDeep and stylish: a burgundy jumper or tie with navy.
Navy + PinkSoft pink lifts navy and suits most skin tones, especially fair and medium.
Navy + MustardComplementary contrast for a confident, autumn look.

Colours to avoid with navy

Black and navy together can look like a mistake unless the black is clearly intentional (e.g. black shoes with a navy suit is fine; a black top with navy trousers often isn't). Very dark green is also too close to navy to read well.

What shoes go with navy?

Brown, tan, white trainers or burgundy loafers. Black works for formal suits.

Does navy suit your skin tone?

Navy is one of the most universally flattering colours. On very dark skin, add a light colour near your face (white, light blue) so the outfit isn't too low-contrast. Check your own colours, then use the Does it match? tool to test any two colours on your skin.

Quick answers

What colour shirt goes with navy pants?

White, light blue, pink, grey, and soft yellow shirts all work with navy trousers. For something bolder, try burgundy or mustard.

Can you wear black with navy?

Yes, but carefully. Black shoes or a black belt with navy are fine. Black and navy as two large pieces (top and trousers) often look mismatched because they're so close in darkness.
