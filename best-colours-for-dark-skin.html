The best colours for dark and brown skin

Short answer: bright, saturated colours look best on dark and brown skin: cobalt blue, emerald, fuchsia, orange, red and mustard. So do crisp white and pastels, which create strong contrast. Be careful with browns and khakis close to your own shade, and with all-black outfits that hide your features.

Why bright colours work

Deep skin has a lot of natural richness, so colours need enough intensity to stand next to it. Muted, dusty shades can make skin look dull. Strong, clear colours make it glow. High contrast (white, bright yellow, cobalt) looks especially sharp.

Colours to be careful with

  • Browns and tans that match your skin. From a distance they blend in and you lose definition. Go lighter (camel, cream) or much darker.
  • Head-to-toe black or navy. It can swallow your features. Break it up with a white tee, a bright shirt or a light jacket.
  • Muddy, greyed-out shades like dull olive or mid grey right next to your face.

Love a colour that isn't on your list? Wear it away from your face, as trousers, shoes or a bag.

Easy outfit ideas

  • White T-shirt + cobalt chinos + white trainers
  • Mustard knit + dark denim + brown boots
  • Emerald shirt or dress + gold jewellery
  • All-cream outfit (cream trousers + cream knit). Simple, but striking on dark skin.
  • Orange or coral top + white jeans for summer

Quick answers

What colours look good on brown skin?

Bright and rich colours such as cobalt blue, emerald green, red, orange, mustard and fuchsia, plus crisp white and cream. Earthy shades like olive and rust also suit warm brown skin.

What colours should dark-skinned people avoid?

Mainly colours very close to your own skin tone, such as mid browns and some khakis, and outfits that are entirely black or navy. They reduce contrast and can make your features harder to see.

Does white look good on dark skin?

Yes. Crisp white is one of the most flattering colours on dark skin because of the strong contrast.
