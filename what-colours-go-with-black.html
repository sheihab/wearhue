Colour matching

What colours go with black?

Short answer: Black goes with almost everything. White is the classic, and grey, camel, red, pink, cobalt and emerald all look great. Bright colours look especially vivid next to black.

7 colours that go with black

Black + WhiteMaximum contrast, always clean. Black jeans with a white tee is a uniform for a reason.
Black + GreyMonochrome and modern. A grey hoodie or coat with black trousers.
Black + CamelBlack and camel is a timeless, expensive-looking pair, especially in coats and knits.
Black + RedBold and classic. Use red as one piece: a top, bag or shoes.
Black + Cobalt blueA brighter alternative to navy that pops against black.
Black + EmeraldRich and elegant, great for evening outfits.
Black + Blush pinkSoftens black, a good balance of edgy and gentle.

Colours to avoid with black

Navy and very dark brown can look muddy next to black. Head-to-toe black is fine, but break it up with texture (denim, knit, leather) so it doesn't look flat.

What shoes go with black?

Black, white trainers, or a bold accent colour. Brown with black is now acceptable in casual outfits.

Does black suit your skin tone?

Fair skin can look washed out in all black near the face; add a lighter layer. Dark skin looks great with black plus a bright or white accent. Check your own colours, then use the Does it match? tool to test any two colours on your skin.

Quick answers

What colour shirt goes with black pants?

White, grey, light blue, pink, red, emerald and camel shirts all work with black trousers. Black on black works too if the fabrics have different textures.

Does brown go with black?

Yes, in casual outfits: a camel or tan coat over black is popular. Mid brown and black can look dull together, so choose a lighter brown.
