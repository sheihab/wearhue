What colours go together? A simple guide to matching clothes

Short answer: pair one colour with neutrals (white, black, grey, navy, beige, denim), and keep outfits to 2–3 main colours. Colours next to each other on the colour wheel blend easily. Opposites (blue + orange, purple + yellow) make a bold statement.

The 5 rules that cover almost every outfit

1. One colour + neutrals always works

Pick one coloured piece (a green tee, a red jumper) and keep everything else neutral: white, black, grey, navy, beige, brown or denim.

2. Stick to 2–3 colours

More than three main colours usually looks unplanned. Neutrals and denim don't count towards the limit.

3. Neighbouring colours blend

Colours next to each other on the colour wheel, like blue + green or red + orange, look naturally harmonious.

4. Opposites make a statement

Complementary colours (blue + orange, purple + yellow, red + green) are bold. Make one the main colour and use the other as a small accent.

5. Mix light and dark

One lighter and one darker piece reads better than two colours of the same brightness, which can look flat.

Quick answers

What colours go together in an outfit?

One colour plus neutrals always works. Beyond that, neighbouring colours on the colour wheel blend naturally, and opposite colours make a bold statement when one is used as an accent.

How many colours should an outfit have?

Two to three main colours. Neutrals like white, black, grey, navy, beige and denim don't count towards that.

What colour goes with everything?

Neutrals: white, black, grey, navy, beige and denim go with almost any colour.
